The daily recap of French government activity ("veille gouvernementale") is a
fourth flow of the existing bot, not a second bot. A timer inside the bot's
process posts one recap a day around 9h (Paris) in #laidé-bas-📢 (channel
550408292820582430). A second bot would need its own Discord application and
token, its own PM2 process and dotenvx secrets, and a copy of the Hermes =-Q=
wrapper and its parser. It would also break the rule that the bot runs as
exactly one process. The existing bot already has the Discord connection, the
Hermes wrapper and the deploy harness.

The data is fetched by code from official open data, and Hermes only writes the
French text from the facts it is given. It never browses government sites
(Légifrance and info.gouv.fr block bots, and the web toolset is unreliable).
Sources and their access details are in [[file:../research/french-gov-activity-sources.org][the research note]].

* Consequences
- *v1 sources:* the day's Journal officiel from DILA's =JORFSIMPLE= night file
  (anonymous, no token), filtered to ordonnances, lois and décrets. Routine
  arrêtés and individual nominations are dropped. The Conseil des ministres
  compte rendu comes from the Élysée RSS feed plus its compte-rendu page.
- *One post a day, at 9h.* The recap on day D covers the Journal officiel of D
  plus any Conseil des ministres published since the previous recap. The Conseil
  usually comes out around 17h, so a Wednesday Conseil appears in Thursday's
  recap.
- *Quiet days:* when nothing is left after filtering, across all sources, the
  bot stays silent. Wednesday is the exception: it posts one short French
  sentence with a mild joke that the government has done nothing worth
  reporting. Hermes writes it each time, with no named individuals.
- *Factual only:* every item links to its official source. No commentary on the
  government's positions.
- *No Journal officiel on Mondays,* so Mondays are usually silent.
- *Restarts:* the date of the last posted recap is kept on disk next to the
  existing caches. A PM2 restart or container recreate neither posts twice nor
  skips a day.
- *Later phases, out of scope for v1:* an evening Conseil des ministres post,
  the Légifrance API (PISTE), Assemblée nationale and Sénat bills,
  vie-publique.fr speeches.
